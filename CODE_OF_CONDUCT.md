# Code of conduct

Be kind. Assume good intent. Help people who are new to ADB and Android TV; everyone started somewhere.

- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- No posting other people's personal info, and be careful with your own: no IP addresses, Wi-Fi names, emails,
  serial numbers or home details in issues and screenshots.
- Keep discussions about the project.

Problems can be reported privately through the repo's
[security reporting](https://github.com/PRODHOSH/fuji-home/security/advisories/new) page. Maintainers may edit,
hide or remove anything that breaks these rules.
